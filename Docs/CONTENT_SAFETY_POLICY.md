# Family Force — Content Safety Policy

This policy is mandatory for every character, animation, stage, prop, UI element, prompt, and Customer Pack.

## Required

- Keep the game suitable for children and families.
- Use modest, loose clothing. Chests, abdomens, shoulders, and legs through the ankles must be covered.
- Keep combat stylized and non-graphic. Communicate hits with animation, impact flashes, hit-pause, sound, and knockback.
- Keep the world religion-neutral. The game must not claim to depict or represent Islam or any other religion.

## Prohibited

- Nudity, revealing or tight clothing, sexualization, or suggestive poses.
- Tattoos, body art, or skin markings on any playable character, companion, enemy, boss, or civilian.
- Crosses, religious crescents, religious buildings, scripture, rituals, clergy, worship imagery, or advocacy for any religion.
- Alcohol, drugs, tobacco, vaping, intoxicants, related containers, branding, or paraphernalia.
- Blood, gore, dismemberment, realistic wounds, corpses, torture, or cruelty.
- Firearms, realistic blades, explosives, or weapons presented with excessive violence.

## Allowed combat language

Simple cartoon-like environmental props such as a bat, pipe, traffic cone, crate, or skateboard may be used without blood or visible injury. Their effects must remain non-graphic.

## Asset acceptance checklist

Before importing or shipping an asset, verify all of the following:

1. Clothing and pose are family-safe and modest.
2. No tattoos or body markings are visible.
3. No religious symbol, wording, architecture, or ritual is present.
4. No alcohol, drugs, smoking, vaping, or related branding is present.
5. No blood, gore, realistic injury, or excessive weapon violence is present.
6. Transparent sprites use a real alpha channel and contain no dark or checkerboard background fringe.

Any asset that fails one item is rejected until corrected.
